## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the quiet authority, heritage, and meticulous craftsmanship of an elite bespoke jewelry atelier and bullion merchant. Rejecting aggressive e-commerce patterns and digital gimmicks, it channels the poise of high-fashion print editorial—invoking the tactility of heavy-stock uncoated rag paper, warm polished vitrines, and archival goldsmithing records.

### Visual Aesthetic & Movements
- **High-End Editorial Minimalism:** Expansive negative space, razor-sharp alignment, strictly balanced typographic hierarchy, and museum-grade presentation of individual works.
- **Architectural Classicism:** Strict rectilinear framing grounded by whisper-light hairlines (1px), subtle structural divisions, and uncompromising geometric balance.
- **Organic Warmth:** The digital experience avoids clinical stark whites and cold industrial slates. Instead, surfaces breathe with champagne ivory, spun silk alabaster, deep volcanic charcoal, and warm antique brass accents.
- **Anti-Trend Permanence:** Completely devoid of noisy glassmorphism, heavy saturated gradients, neon glows, or playful bouncy micro-interactions. Surfaces are flat, dignified, and tactile.

## Colors

The chromatic identity is deliberately subdued to ensure physical precious metals, rare gemstones, and bullion ingots remain the vibrant center of visual weight.

### Palette Architecture
- **Surface & Canvas:**
  - `Base Canvas (#FBF9F5)`: The foundational warm champagne ivory; non-glaring and luminous.
  - `Surface Container (#F5F0EB)`: Alabaster cream for cards, collection bays, and elevated modules.
  - `Surface Accent (#EEE7DF)`: Recessed areas, category pill backdrops, and subtle table striping.
- **Typography & Structural Lines:**
  - `Primary Ink (#1A1816)`: Deep charcoal obsidian. Used for primary editorial headings, titles, and high-emphasis text, offering rich contrast without the severity of pure black.
  - `Secondary Ink (#57524C)`: Warm taupe-graphite for editorial body, certifications, and specifications.
  - `Muted Inscriptions (#8C857B)`: Used for uppercase track-leaded category monikers and metadata.
  - `Structural Hairline (#EAE4DC)`: A 1px architectural divider tone defining card edges, headers, and footer bands.
- **Metallic & Accent Roles:**
  - `Antique Brass / Gold (#B89047)`: Reserved for focused CTAs, refined badges, price emphasis, and active indicators. Never used in large garish fills.
  - `Deep Burnished Gold (#8C6A2E)`: Hover states, active borders, and prestigious hallmark stamps.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic haute editorial serif and contemporary architectural clarity.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Styling
- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the narrative weight. Headline titles feature classical high contrast and elegant proportions. Italicized flourishes are intentionally deployed for emotive focal words (e.g., *Brilliance*, *Elegance*, *Heritage*) to evoke master atelier custom-engraving.
- **Informational Precision (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its modern, balanced geometry and supreme legibility at small scale. It powers bullion weight metrics, metal purities, stone dimensions, and descriptive product narratives.
- **Eyebrow & Label Rules:** Subtitles, pre-headers, and navigation utility items must strictly be formatted in uppercase with an expansive tracking scale (`letter-spacing: 0.12em` to `0.18em`), anchoring the luxury house aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts rely on measured cadence and breathing room. Content is not compressed to fit above the fold; instead, it unfolds with the cadence of an expansive art catalogue.

### Grid & Architecture
- **Desktop Grid:** 12-column symmetrical fluid grid bounded to a generous max-width of `1440px`. Exterior margins expand to `margin-desktop (4rem)` to isolate content from peripheral distraction. Column gutters are set to `gutter-desktop (2.5rem)` for airy gallery cards.
- **Tablet Grid:** 8-column layout with `2rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins. Curated grids transition gracefully from 4 columns to 2.
- **Mobile Grid:** 4-column layout with `gutter (1.5rem)` and `margin (1.5rem)` edges. Multi-column showcase cards transition into deliberate single-focus cards or clean horizontal rail carousels.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Section blocks are separated by generous vertical expanses (`4rem` to `7rem`), accented with centered 1px ornamental separation lines or hallmark monograms.
- Component-level spacing uses `space-md` for text-to-label offsets and `space-xl` for inner vitrine padding.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids simulated software drop-shadows and blurred multi-tiered elevations. It relies on architectural layering and surface tonal contrast.

### Planar Depth
- **Zero Heavy Shadows:** Standard cards and containers use zero box-shadow. Visual separation is accomplished exclusively through the 1px structural hairlines (`#EAE4DC`) and transitions between `#FBF9F5` and `#F5F0EB`.
- **Atmospheric Float:** On delicate interactive states (such as hovering over an individual diamond solitaire card), apply a whisper-light ambient glow: `0 8px 30px rgba(26, 24, 22, 0.04)`. The shadow is warm-toned and imperceptible, mimicking the soft natural dissipation of warm gallery spot illumination.
- **Modal & Drawer Overlays:** Private consultation dialogs and high-value inquiry drawers enter smoothly over a tinted backdrop of `rgba(26, 24, 22, 0.45)` with a minimal 2px backdrop blur, ensuring jewelry images behind remain recognizable yet subdued.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary honors the sharp cuts of emeralds, step baguettes, and bullion bars.

### Form Treatment
- **Sharp Rectilinear Lines (`roundedness: 0`):** Outer button perimeters, product display vitrines, search containers, and media modules possess clean 90-degree corners.
- **Intentional Organic Exceptions:** Small circular indicators (such as diamond carat size selectors or pagination points) and oval circular category thumbnails may exist strictly as pure architectural circles (`50% border-radius`), contrasting against the crisp rectangular framework.
- **Border Detailing:** All decorative and structural borders are locked to a delicate `1px` uniform thickness (`#EAE4DC`). Heavy framing is forbidden.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Links
- **Primary Atelier Action:** Sharp rectangular button filled with Antique Brass (`#B89047`) or Dark Charcoal (`#1A1816`). Text is set in uppercase `label-button` typography with crisp letter spacing in contrasting ivory (`#FBF9F5`). Hover subtly transitions to Deep Burnished Gold (`#8C6A2E`) or deep obsidian with micro-expansion transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, crisp 1px solid border (`#1A1816` or `#B89047`), and matching colored ink.
- **Editorial Text Link:** Understated typography followed by a slender directional glyph (`→`), accented with a delicate underline positioned 4px beneath the text baseline.

### Product Showcase Cards (Vitrines)
- **Structure:** Encased in flat `#F5F0EB` surfaces with optional 1px `#EAE4DC` architectural boundary borders.
- **Imagery:** Generous ivory padding surrounding high-resolution product photography featuring natural drop-shadows photographed in studio.
- **Details:** Centered typography stack consisting of eyebrow collection names in uppercase muted graphite, title in `Playfair Display`, and price display paired with metal purity badges (e.g., `18k Yellow Gold`, `999.9 Fine Bullion`).
- **Utility Actions:** Discrete wishlist hearts and quick-inquire icons positioned seamlessly in top corners, styled with 1px stroke geometry without background pills.

### Chips, Filter Pills & Badges
- **Editorial Category Tags:** Rectilinear or subtly pill-curved minimal tags in `#EEE7DF` background with `#1A1816` text. Active state shifts to a solid Dark Charcoal fill with `#FBF9F5` type.
- **Hallmark Badges:** Delicate 1px outlined badges with subtle brass foil coloration denoting bespoke certification (e.g., *GIA Certified*, *Ethically Sourced*, *LBMA Accredited*).

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Fields:** Flat inputs in `#FFFFFF` or `#FBF9F5` bordered with 1px solid `#EAE4DC`. 0px rounded corners.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts smoothly to Antique Brass (`#B89047`) with zero default browser outline ring.
- **Placeholder & Labels:** Floating or top-aligned micro labels set in uppercase `label-caption` with expanded tracking.

### Trust, Provenance & Value Pillars
- Horizontal striping with balanced five-column arrangement. Minimalist 1px hairline icon outlines (e.g., certified scales, gem facets, wax seals, gift box) paired with centered two-line descriptions in `body-sm`.

### Bespoke Consultations & Ingot Modules
- Rich charcoal background panels (`#1A1816`) with ivory serif headlines, fine brass accent lines, and streamlined single-action booking inputs.
